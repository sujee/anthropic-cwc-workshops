Events, as they arrive

    What the agent concluded

    It read the runbook first

    The first thing the agent did was read SKILL.md in its sandbox. The skill steered the order: deploys, then metrics, then the diff, and the log last.

    It paused and waited for you

    Every call to get_metrics, get_recent_deploys or get_diff stopped the agent (status idle, reason requires_action) until your handle_tool() replied.

    The log never reached the model

    The log has about 70,000 lines. The agent searched it with two bash calls inside the sandbox, and only their output came back.
